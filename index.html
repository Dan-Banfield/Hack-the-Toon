What’s the craic?

Hack the Toon is Newcastle’s student hackathon: a weekend where you team up with strangers, learn something new, and build a thing that didn’t exist on Saturday morning.

Never coded before? Championgreat, excellent. We run beginner workshops on the first morning and our mentors are there all night. Been to fifty hackathons? Grand — we’ve got prizes worth showing off.

Frequently asked questions. If yours isn’t here, just ask us.

Who can come?

Any student at a UK university or college, plus anyone who graduated in the last year. You need to be 18 or over on the day.

How much does it cost?

Nowt. Entry, food, drink and swag are all free, thanks to our sponsors.

I’ve never been to a hackathon. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Plenty of people will be at their very first one. There are beginner workshops, mentors on hand all night, and a prize just for new teams.

Do I need a team?

Teams are up to four people. Come with mates, or turn up solo and join our team-forming session on Saturday morning.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, student ID, toothbrush and something warm. It’s Newcastle in February and the locals will still be in T-shirts.

How do I get there?

Hop on the Metro. We’re a short walk from the station, and we’ll share the exact venue with confirmed hackers. Travel bursaries are available for students coming from further afield.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. In short: gan cannytake care, be careful and be kind. Harassment of any kind isn’t tolerated. The full code of conduct will be published before registration closes.
